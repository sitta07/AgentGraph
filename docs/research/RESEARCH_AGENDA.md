# Research agenda — open, testable questions

This list is ranked by **scientific value × feasibility ÷ cost** on one laptop
(8 cores). Every entry names its falsifier. The status column is updated as
studies complete.

| # | Question | Why it is open | Prediction / falsifier | Cost | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Can social behaviour (aggression, sharing) be *learned within one lifetime* by reward-modulated spiking agents? | Few studies test social behaviour in biologically-plausible learners. | Pre-registered H1–H4 | ~1 min | **done: no** ([findings](FINDINGS_SOCIAL.md)) |
| 2 | **Does selfish lifetime learning undermine kin-selected helping?** | Kin selection theory (Hamilton; Taylor 1992) and the Baldwin effect are rarely put in tension. We found no study of this with spiking, plastic agents. | H5: viscosity raises innate share drive. H6: learning lowers realised sharing. H7: no kin-directed sharing without kin recognition. | ~15 min | **done: H6 falsified (learning doubles sharing), H5 did not replicate, H7 null rejected** ([C](FINDINGS_EVOLUTION_KIN.md), [C2](FINDINGS_C2.md)); follow-ups F, F2, G, I, J |
| 3 | Does a heritable aggression gene evolve upward under scarcity? | Resource-defence theory predicts aggression is highest at intermediate or clumped resources, not maximal scarcity. | Aggression gene: scarce vs abundant. Falsified if no difference or the reverse. | ~20 min | **done: no** (D's rise did not replicate in D2 against a neutral marker; [findings](FINDINGS_D.md)) |
| 4 | Do signals evolve *meaning*? | Referential communication has emerged in evolved SNN swarms (arXiv 2212.08484). Whether it does under competition, not cooperation, is less clear. | Mutual information I(signal; signaller's food/threat sensors) > shuffled control; receivers change behaviour after hearing. | ~30 min | **done: no** in this world ([M](FINDINGS_M.md)); with a reason to communicate: S running; S (food needs a partner, directional hearing): still no meaning, although the partner rule did bind ([S](FINDINGS_S.md)) |
| 5 | Baldwin effect in spiking agents: does plasticity speed up the evolution of innate foraging? | A classic result (Hinton & Nowlan 1987). It has rarely been measured with reward-modulated STDP. | Innate foraging score vs generation: learning on vs off. Falsified if slopes are equal. | ~20 min | **done: hiding effect, not Baldwin** ([findings](FINDINGS_E.md)) |
| 6 | Darwinian vs Lamarckian inheritance of learned weights. | Lamarckian inheritance is faster in static worlds but may be brittle in changing ones. | Adaptation after an environment shift. | ~20 min | **inconclusive**: the shift test failed its manipulation check ([N](FINDINGS_N.md)); Lamarckian populations go extinct far more often ([N2](FINDINGS_N2.md)); N3 shift test failed again, Lamarckian died sooner a third time ([N3](FINDINGS_N3.md)) |
| 7 | Can RL policies (PettingZoo) invade a population of SNN agents, and vice versa? | Mixed populations of hand-coded, learned and evolved controllers are rarely compared under identical rules. | Relative population share over time. | variable | **done: no advantage either way**; lineage fate all-or-nothing ([O](FINDINGS_O.md)) |
| 8 | Does spatial structure (offspring placement) protect evolving populations from extinction? | Found by accident in C2. | Well-mixed vs viscous extinction and survival time. | ~80 min | **done: delays, does not prevent** ([F + F2](FINDINGS_F.md)); mechanism open |
| 9 | Is the learned rise in social actions specific to reward, or a side effect of the learning rule? | E and G left ~80% of the effect unexplained. | I: shared credit; J: frozen locomotion. | ~35 min each | **done: side effect of locomotion weakening** ([I](FINDINGS_I.md), [J](FINDINGS_J.md)) |
| 10 | Why do locomotion weights fall during learning, and is it the *weakening* (not other foraging changes) that frees social actions? | J froze all locomotion learning at once. | Row-normalise locomotion inputs only: foraging learning stays, total drive is fixed. Falsified if social rates still rise. | ~35 min | **done: yes, the weakening** ([K](FINDINGS_K.md)); driven by negative RPEs ([P](FINDINGS_P.md)); fix tested in R |
| 11 | What collapses first in well-mixed populations: food, attacks or reproduction? | F2 found no difference in volatility before collapse (exploratory). | Cause-of-death and birth-rate time series before extinction. | ~80 min | **done** ([L](FINDINGS_L.md)): lower birth rate and more starvation before collapse; kin vs density in Q |
| 12 | Does viscosity help through kinship or local density? | L ruled out food patches. | Q: newborns next to a stranger vs parent vs anywhere. | ~90 min | **inconclusive** ([Q](FINDINGS_Q.md)); post hoc, stranger placement 11/12 extinct vs parent 3/12 → kinship lead; Q2: replicated (12/12 vs 6/12), but parents, not strangers, kill newborns (42% of kills) ; Q3: energy recycling falsified ([Q3](FINDINGS_Q3.md)); pooled 34/36 vs 13/36; Q4: gradual spread not supported, but newborns placed just 0.15 from the parent went extinct 8/12 vs 2/12, with no further effect at 0.45 ([Q4](FINDINGS_Q4.md)); Q5: **parent-specific** — beside another relative 10/12 extinct vs beside the parent 2/12 ([Q5](FINDINGS_Q5.md)); Q6: parental culling falsified (2/12 vs 3/12 extinct without filial attacks, [Q6](FINDINGS_Q6.md)); next: does the newborn absorb aggression (Q7)? |
| 13 | Does a stimulus-specific critic remove the learning bias? | Frémaux et al. 2010 predict it should. | R: critic per sensor state vs scalar. | ~25 min | **done: mostly yes** ([R](FINDINGS_R.md)); foraging cost halved, not removed |
| 14 | Does Lamarckian inheritance compound the bias? | Post-hoc in N. | N2, fresh seeds, with a state-critic arm. | ~60 min | **done** ([N2](FINDINGS_N2.md)): Lamarckian extinction replicated (9/12 vs 1/12); state critic does not prevent it; 2/12 state-critic runs found a peaceful high-food state |
| 15 | A better learning rule for social worlds: does lifetime learning ever pay in evolving populations? | P: learners eat ~31% less. | Compare rules (state critic, symmetric STDP, action-specific eligibility) on food_rate and survival. | ~1 h | **done: no rule pays yet** ([T](FINDINGS_T.md)); best is state critic + low lr at −7%; needs a world with something to learn (item 16) |
| 16 | Environment-shift test done properly | N's shift was too weak and was measured too late. | Repeated shifts; food_rate time series right after each shift. | ~1 h | **failed manipulation check** ([N3](FINDINGS_N3.md)): left/right flips do not lower foraging; needs a shift validated by pilot |
| 17 | Do evolving agents use their senses to find food at all? | Three shift tests (N, N3, swap pilot) failed their manipulation checks; evolved weights showed no sensory structure. | V: scramble the food/hazard sensors; falsified if real senses do not raise foraging. | ~50 min | **done: no measurable use** ([V](FINDINGS_V.md)); exploratory follow-up points to the decision dynamics (lock-in, 8 ms window, runaway potentiation) |
| 18 | Can lifetime learning pay if the decision dynamics are fixed (32 ms window, state cleared, normalised weights)? | V follow-up probes: lock-in, short window, runaway potentiation. | W: single agents, each fix ablated; falsified if trained ≤ untrained. | ~15 min | **done: yes, 5.7×, all three fixes necessary** ([W](FINDINGS_W.md)); social worlds open (mass killing in pilot) |

## Principles

- Pre-register the hypotheses and the falsifier before running.
- Multiple seeds, paired designs where possible, and 95% bootstrap CIs.
- Commit the raw results (`results/`) and the code version that produced them.
- Report negative results with the same care as positive ones.
