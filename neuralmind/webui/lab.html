Discovery Lab

Each card is a study whose hypotheses and pass/fail rules were fixed before it ran. Results show the mean effect over seeds with a 95% confidence interval, and negative results stay on the board.

How to read a card

  • Dot is the mean difference between conditions. Bar is its 95% confidence interval. The thin line is zero.
  • Green: the whole interval is above zero. Red: the whole interval is below zero. Blue: it crosses zero, so no clear effect.
  • A hypothesis counts as supported only if its interval excludes zero in the predicted direction.
